How it works

Three steps to your own store.

Launch in minutes, not months. We handle the hard parts.

1. Curate your catalog

Upload products with beautiful photos, set prices, and organize into collections. Our editor makes it feel like arranging a boutique window.

  • Bulk CSV import
  • Auto image optimization
  • Variants for size, color, fit

2. Share everywhere

Send your catalog link via WhatsApp, Instagram, or email. Customers browse and order without leaving their favorite app.

  • One-tap WhatsApp sharing
  • Instagram catalog sync
  • QR codes for offline stores

3. Fulfill with confidence

Track orders, print labels, and manage returns — all in one dashboard. We integrate with every major Indian courier.

  • Auto label generation
  • Real-time tracking
  • Return management
