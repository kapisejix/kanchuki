| Follow-up 5 — a floor under each signal | `socialProofChip()` now gates on two exported constants instead of `> 0`: **`MIN_VIEWED_CHIP = 3`** and **`MIN_FAVORITED_CHIP = 1`**. The asymmetry is the design, not a tuning nicety — a save is deliberate, so one is real evidence; a view is passive, so one is indistinguishable from noise and a chip reading "1 viewed today" argues against the product. Equalising them would either hide genuine single saves or re-ship the exact chip the floor exists to prevent, so the relationship is asserted as a test (`MIN_VIEWED_CHIP > MIN_FAVORITED_CHIP`) rather than left to a comment. |
| The floor's real ceiling, recorded rather than smoothed over | The rollup stores only each day's **top 10**, so a small count means the store had a quiet day — it is not random noise. In a low-traffic boutique that means most counts are small and any floor suppresses **most** chips, which is the intended trade under "evidence only, never a placeholder" but should be tuned from the real distribution (a read-only `jsonb_array_elements` histogram over `retailer_engagement_daily`) rather than from taste; the constant's own comment says so. A refresh would therefore add requests and a new failure path for zero visible benefit.
